ChillScenes

Relaxing scenes and sleep sounds for Apple TV

ChillScenes turns your Apple TV into a window to peaceful places. Pick a scene, shape the sound the way you like it, and let your TV set the mood while you read, work, have dinner or drift off to sleep.

Every scene is generated in real time on your Apple TV with Metal. These aren't looping videos: the motion never repeats the same way, there are no jumps, nothing to buffer, and it all works offline.

View on the App Store

16 scenes in three collections

  • Nature: Ocean, Clouds, Sparks, River, Beach, Desert, Flock and Northern Lights.
  • Indoors: Road, Fire, Rain on Glass and Snowy Window.
  • Motion: Vaporfuji, Ghost Text, Starry Night and Neon Flower.

A sound mixer in every scene

Each scene has its own soundscape, and you decide how it sounds:

  • Two continuous ambient layers, each with its own volume.
  • Two occasional sounds that play at random, like distant thunder or a crackling log.
  • Choose whether occasional sounds come rarely, at a normal pace or often.

Made for sleep

The sleep timer slowly fades the picture to black and the sound to silence. Then your Apple TV can go to sleep as usual.

Automatic scene changes

ChillScenes can switch to a random scene every few minutes with smooth transitions. Or stay with your favorite as long as you like.

Designed for the Siri Remote

  • Open the app and your last scene starts right away.
  • Simple controls with the Liquid Glass design of tvOS 26.
  • In-app brightness control for late nights or bright living rooms.
  • Available in 20 languages.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking and no data collection. Your settings stay on your Apple TV. Details in the privacy policy.
