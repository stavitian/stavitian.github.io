Highlights

  • 16 scenes in three collections. Nature (Ocean, Clouds, Sparks, River, Beach, Desert, Flock and Northern Lights), Indoors (Road, Fire, Rain on Glass and Snowy Window) and Motion (Vaporfuji, Ghost Text, Starry Night and Neon Flower).
  • Rendered in real time. Every scene is an original shader computed on your device: no looping videos, the motion never repeats the same way, and it works offline.
  • A sound mixer in every scene. Two continuous ambient layers and two occasional sounds, each with its own volume, and you choose how often they play.
  • Sleep timer. The picture fades to black and the sound to silence, gently.
  • Automatic scene changes with smooth transitions, brightness control and 20 languages.
