Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

ChillScenes is an app for Apple TV, iPhone, iPad and Android made by Santiago Tavitian. This policy covers every version of the app.

Summary

ChillScenes does not collect, store or share any personal data. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

The App Privacy section of our App Store listing reads: “Data Not Collected”, and the Data safety section of our Google Play listing states that no data is collected or shared.

What data we process

  • None. The app does not send any information to our servers or to anyone else's. It works offline.
  • App settings (last scene, mixer volumes, brightness, timers, language) are stored only on your device so the app can remember your preferences. They never leave the device and we cannot access them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Third-party SDKs

ChillScenes contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or social media SDKs. On Apple devices it only uses Apple's system frameworks. On Android it only uses the Android system libraries and Google's Play Billing Library, which is needed to process the purchase.

Purchases

The one-time purchase that unlocks all scenes and the sound library is handled entirely by the store you buy from: Apple through the App Store on Apple devices, and Google through Google Play on Android. We never receive or store your name, email, payment method or any other purchase details. The app only asks the store whether the purchase is active so it can unlock the content. That data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Store statistics

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple or Google may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as installs or crash reports). You control this in your device settings, and this data does not identify you.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not process personal data, we hold no data about you to access, correct or delete. If you have any privacy question, just write to us. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the date above before releasing that version.
