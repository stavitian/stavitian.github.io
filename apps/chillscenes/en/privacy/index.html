Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

ChillScenes is an Apple TV app made by [NOMBRE DEL DESARROLLADOR].

Summary

ChillScenes does not collect, store or share any personal data. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

The App Privacy section of our App Store listing reads: “Data Not Collected”.

What data we process

  • None. The app does not send any information to our servers or to anyone else's. It works offline.
  • App settings (last scene, mixer volumes, brightness, timers, language) are stored only on your Apple TV so the app can remember your preferences. They never leave the device and we cannot access them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Third-party SDKs

ChillScenes contains no third-party SDKs: no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or social media SDKs. It only uses Apple's system frameworks.

Purchases

The purchase that unlocks all scenes and the sound library is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your name, email, payment method or any other purchase details. The app only asks Apple's system whether the purchase is active so it can unlock the content. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Apple statistics

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your Apple TV settings, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as crash reports). You control this in your device settings, and this data does not identify you.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not process personal data, we hold no data about you to access, correct or delete. If you have any privacy question, just write to us. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the date above before releasing that version.
