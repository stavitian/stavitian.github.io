Support

Frequently asked questions about ChillScenes for Apple TV, iPhone, iPad and Android.

Which scenes are free?
Clouds and Road, with no time limit and with their default sound mix.
What does the purchase include?
It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks all 16 scenes and the full sound library: in the sound mix you can choose any base sound or event for each scene. Without it you can still adjust each sound’s volume and how often events play.
How do I restore my purchase?
Pick a locked scene (or try to change a sound in the sound mix) to open the unlock sheet and choose Restore Purchases.
  • Apple TV, iPhone and iPad (App Store): use the same Apple Account you bought it with.
  • Android (Google Play): make sure Google Play is using the same Google Account you bought it with. The purchase is also recognized when the app opens; if it doesn’t show up, tap Restore Purchases while connected to the internet. You can see your purchases in Google Play › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Budget & history.
Purchases don’t carry over between stores: a purchase made on the App Store doesn’t unlock the app on Android, and vice versa.
Can I share the purchase with my family?
On the App Store, yes: the purchase supports Family Sharing, so members of your family group can unlock the scenes with their own account. On Google Play, in-app purchases can’t be shared through Family Library.
How do I change scenes?
On Apple TV, while a scene is playing, swipe or press left or right on the remote and click to open the menu with every scene and the options. On iPhone, iPad and Android, swipe left or right on the screen and tap to open the menu.
How does the sleep timer work?
On the start screen, pick a duration from 15 minutes to 8 hours, or No limit. When it ends, the picture fades to black and the sound to silence. Your device can then go to sleep according to its system setting. Press any button on the remote (or tap the screen) to bring the scene back.
Can it change scenes on its own?
Yes. In Options › Random scene, turn it on and choose how often (every 3 to 30 minutes). The app switches to a random scene with a smooth transition.
Can I lower the brightness?
Yes, in Options › Brightness. Handy at night or in a dark room.
Which languages does it support?
20 languages. It follows your device language by default; you can choose another one in Options › Language without restarting the app.
Does it need an internet connection?
No. The scenes and sounds are built into the app and work offline. Only the store (App Store or Google Play) needs a connection to buy or restore.
Does it collect any data?
No. No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your settings are stored only on your device. See the privacy policy.
What do I need to use it?
An Apple TV with tvOS 26 or later, an iPhone with iOS 26 or later, an iPad with iPadOS 26 or later, or an Android device with Android 8.0 or later.
